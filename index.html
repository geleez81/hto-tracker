HTO Tracker Documentation & Guide

Protected View-Only Mode: By default, this tracker prevents accidental edits, additions, or date alterations.

Search & Filter: Use the search bar to locate any employee by name. Use the flat department dropdown to filter groups (T1MSG, T1VIN, SMM, T2, Back Office, QA, etc.).

Balance Formula: Remaining credits are calculated dynamically: Remaining = Total Credits - (Scheduled Dates + DVO Alert Strikes)

Export: Anyone can click Export CSV at any time to download the current filtered view.

Unlocking Edit Mode: Click the "View Only" button and authenticate with the password.

Scheduling Dates: Click + Add Date on any member row, pick or type the full date, enter your GMSCC Name, and click Save Date.

DVO Alert Strikes: Use the + / - stepper under "Alert: No Answer" to log operational strikes. This is active exclusively for DVO groups (T1MSG, T1VIN, SMM, T2).

Duplicate Protection: The tracker prevents adding duplicate names during manual entry or roster uploads.

Data Isolation: Adding or removing personnel will never recalculate or disrupt other employees' scheduled dates or balances.
